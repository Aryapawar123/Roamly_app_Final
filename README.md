# 🌍 Roamly — Travel Smart, Not Hard

> **“Travel plans should adapt to humans — not the other way around.”**

Travel is exciting.  
Planning it? Not always.

Most travelers juggle **5–6 different apps** for itineraries, navigation, bookings, expenses, and memories. Even then, plans often break when reality hits — late flights, long café breaks, traffic, or group disagreements.

**Roamly** solves this.

Roamly is a **reality-aware adaptive travel companion** that dynamically adjusts your travel plan based on **real-world conditions and human behavior.**

Instead of forcing travelers to follow rigid schedules, **Roamly bends the itinerary to match your day.**

---

# ✨ What Makes Roamly Special

### 🧠 Reality-Aware Itinerary

Your travel plans automatically adapt if:

- you start late
- traffic delays you
- you decide to stay longer at a place

No more constant re-planning.

---

### ⚡ Energy-Based Planning

Not every travel day feels the same.

Roamly lets you choose the pace of your trip:

- 🧘 **Relaxed** → Leisurely exploration with breaks  
- ⚖ **Balanced** → Perfect mix of activity and rest  
- 🚀 **Packed** → Maximize every hour of the day  

Your **energy decides the itinerary.**

---

### 👥 Smart Group Travel

Group trips often turn chaotic. Roamly keeps everyone aligned.

Features include:

- Role-based access (Planner, Viewer, Expense Manager)
- Live voting for activities
- Preference balancing
- Real-time itinerary updates

No more endless group chat debates.

---

### 💰 Integrated Expense Tracking

Track and manage travel finances effortlessly.

- Split bills instantly
- Track group spending
- Monitor trip budgets
- Identify cost vs time tradeoffs

Travel together without **money confusion.**

---

### 📍 Smart Navigation & Travel Helper

Roamly helps you move efficiently.

Get insights like:

- cheapest transport options
- fastest routes
- nearby cafés, ATMs, or attractions
- crowd-aware suggestions

Your **smart travel assistant on the go.**

---

### 📸 Memory Timeline

Your trip automatically becomes a **story.**

Roamly captures:

- location-tagged photos
- day-wise memories
- timeline of places visited

Turn your journey into a **digital travel diary.**

---

### 🛟 Offline Survival Mode

Travel doesn’t stop when the internet does.

Roamly still provides:

- offline maps
- emergency contacts
- nearby help centers
- essential travel info

Because safety should never depend on connectivity.

# 🧩 System Architecture

Roamly integrates multiple components to deliver a seamless travel experience:

- **Flutter mobile application**
- **Node.js backend services**
- **Firebase for authentication & real-time data**
- **Google Maps APIs for navigation & location intelligence**
- **Cloud storage for photos and trip data**

This architecture ensures **scalability, reliability, and real-time collaboration.**
